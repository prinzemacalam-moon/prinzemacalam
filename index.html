Different Types of Comics

Manga refers to comics and graphic novels that originate from Japan. While the word simply means "comics" in Japanese, outside of Japan it specifically describes Japanese-style comic art. It have Black and White Art and Right to Left Reading. Writer or Artist who created manga are called mangaka.

Manhwa (만화) is the Korean word for comics and print cartoons. In English, it specifically refers to comic books or graphic novels originating from South Korea. Manwha traditionally in printed books, modern manwha has popularized Digital Webtoon. It format have full-color, vertical-scroll strips designed for smartphones. Korean comics are read vertically from top to bottom in a long-strip webtoon format ( left to right direction).

Manhua are Chinese-language comics and graphic novels produced in Greater China (including mainland China, Taiwan, and Hong Kong). Many popular digital manhua adapt Chinese web novels (light novels) and feature genres such as: • Cultivation & Martial Arts • Isekai & Fantasy • Romance & Historical Drama Most modern digital/mainland Chinese manhua use a left to right format similar to Manhwa Webtoons.

To summarize Manga, Manhua and Manhwa are comics the difference is that, Manga, Manhua, Manhwa are the names given to Comics in different languages. (Japanese, Chinese, Korean respectively.) Use of any one of the terms indicates country and context of origin. There is also cultural difference on story telling and the difference of art style between the three of them.

Five Recommendations

Frieren: Beyond Journey’s End

TYPE: MANGA | GENRE: ADVENTURE, COMEDY, DRAMA, SLICE OF LIFE

The adventure is over but life goes on for an elf mage just beginning to learn what living is all about. Elf mage Frieren and her courageous fellow adventurers have defeated the Demon King and brought peace to the land. With the great struggle over, they all go their separate ways to live a quiet life. But as an elf, Frieren, nearly immortal, will long outlive the rest of her former party. How will she come to terms with the mortality of her friends? How can she find fulfillment in her own life, and can she learn to understand what life means to the humans around her? Frieren begins a new journey to find the answer. This manga is great because of great story telling, characters, and meaningful lessons about time. It's a great recommendation for readers who enjoy fantasy adventures with emotional storytelling.

Mairimashita! Iruma-kun

Type: MANGA | GENRE: ADVENTURE, COMEDY, ISEKAI

Welcome to Demon School! Iruma-kun follows Iruma Suzuki, a kindhearted boy whose irresponsible parents sell him to a demon named Sullivan. Instead of being harmed, Iruma is adopted as Sullivan’s grandson and sent to a school in the demon world. He must adjust to his unusual new environment while keeping his human identity a secret. This manga entertaining is its combination of comedy, fantasy, and school adventures. It’s suitable for readers who enjoy diverse characters with development, wholesomeness, comedy and great emotioal growth

No Home

TYPE: MANHWA | GENRE: COMEDY, DRAMA, SLICE OF LIFE

Haejoon Goh, who ran from home, and Eunyung Baek, the problem child who lives in a tent. These two, who met under terrible circumstances, are forced to live together in an abandoned dormitory. This is a story of difficult growth between two people who hate both the dorm and each other. What makes this story so good and interesting is its portrayal of friendship, personal growth, and the meaning of finding a place where you belong. It’s a great read for people who appreciate character development and emotionally meaningful stories.

Omniscient Reader’s Viewpoint

TYPE: MANHWA | GENRE: FANTASY, ACTION, ADVENTURE

Dokja was an average office worker whose sole interest was reading his favorite web novel 'Three Ways to Survive the Apocalypse.' But when the novel suddenly becomes reality, he is the only person who knows how the world will end. Armed with this realization, Dokja uses his understanding to change the course of the story, and the world, as he knows it. What makes this manhwa interesting is its unique and great storyline, unpredictable plot twists, and complex characters. It’s a great recommendation for readers who like action-adventure, apocalypse and Isekai

Nanhao and Shang Feng

TYPE: MANHUA | GENRE: COMEDY, SLICE OF LIFE

Nan Hao & Shang Feng is a gag-slice of life manhua about two ordinary high school boys and their everyday experiences. This series is enjoyable because of its humorous illustrations and exaggerated reactions, which turn even simple everyday situations into entertaining moments. It's a good choice for readers who want something lighthearted and funny without complicated storylines. (Chapters are short)
